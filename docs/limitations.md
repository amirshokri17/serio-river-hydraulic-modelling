# Scope, uncertainty and limitations

- **Academic input provenance:** The input geometry, flood hydrograph, River2D solved state, and course source files were supplied for the modelling exercise and are not redistributed.
- **Field validation:** No observed flood stages or velocities were used to independently calibrate or validate this public analysis. Agreement of model outputs does not establish predictive accuracy.
- **Manning scenarios:** Minimum, nominal, and maximum roughness assignments bound sensitivity within the chosen physical ranges; they are not statistical confidence limits or a verified true channel resistance.
- **River2D solution:** The 2D model was supplied, not re-simulated, numerically converged, or calibrated as part of this work. Displayed Qin = 559.000 and Qout = 578.730 m³/s differ by 19.730 m³/s, **3.53%** of Qin. An explanation has not been established.
- **Mesh extent:** The River2D footprint covers a subset of the 1D reach; S08 has an internal mesh gap. Missing mesh data were not extrapolated.
- **Different velocity definitions:** River2D section-normal depth-averaged local values and HEC-RAS main-channel means are not interchangeable.
- **Bridge and levees:** These structures are represented but no independent with/without comparison was conducted to assign isolated effects.
- **Reproduction:** The seven archived source listings are checksum-verified and syntax-checked. The original complete model and solved outputs are deliberately not published; rerunning them requires legitimate access to the originals and software.
- **Use limitations:** No validated flood extent, design flood stage, erosion prediction, quantitative risk assessment, or safety-critical engineering decision is supported by the public portfolio.

# Third-party material and publication scope

This public repository contains analysis and post-processing code, original summary graphics, and selected derived numerical findings from an academic hydraulic modelling exercise on the lower Serio River.

## Deliberately excluded

The original HEC-RAS geometry and plan files, supplied hydrographs, HDF and DSS outputs, source topographic/section data, River2D mesh and solution files, course workbooks, field photographs, maps, Google Earth imagery, course teaching material, and the full academic report are **not distributed**. They were provided or assembled in the course context and have separate authorship and redistribution considerations.

The small tables in data/processed/ are rounded derived indicators, not raw datasets. The graphics in figures/ are original programmatically drawn layouts from these indicators, not screenshots or third-party geographic imagery.
